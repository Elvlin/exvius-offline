# Exvius Offline v0.1.31

**November 14 update (build 9072)**

- Updates to unit awakening, fusion, unit selling and shop purchases.
- Equipment and party changes stay saved after restarting the game.
- Includes the battle music and missing summon artwork fixes.

Open the game online to get the update prompt. Install over your current game to keep your save. Do not uninstall it.

**Earlier fixes included in this update**

- Restored battle music.
- Fixed missing artwork when browsing summon seasons.
- The game gets the required data before starting.

**Fixes**

- **Corrected Ramuh’s quest reward.** Completing his quest now grants Ramuh instead of Carbuncle.
- **Fixed earned esper dungeons disappearing after restarting.** Existing saves can recover missing esper unlocks without paying the required items again.
- **Restored 25 missing Chamber of the Fallen equipment rewards**, including Cursed Doll. Previously cleared battles can award the missing equipment on another successful clear without duplicating rewards.
- **Added protection against Vortex crashes when a banner has no playable stages**, including events whose daily attempts have been used.
- **Corrected Equipment Enhancement dungeon routing.**
- **Corrected a mismatch between the summon banner data and its index.**

**Summons and Shops**

- **Updated Standard Summon to Standard Summon+.** Added previously mailbox-only units, with an equal chance for each unit in the expanded pool.
- **Restored 10x summoning for Standard Summon+**, costing 2,000 Friend Points.
- **Restored the original Gil Shop** with 48 items, including awakening materials and throwing items, using their documented Gil prices.
- **Adjusted VIP and EX Shop prices:** 10 Lapis per original VIP Coin and 5 Lapis per original EX Coin.
- **Rebalanced Daily Coin Shop prices.** Limited stock resets daily.
- **Removed purchase limits from the VIP, EX and Gil Shops.**
- **Corrected missing shop names and descriptions**, including entries displaying internal text codes.

**Menus and Display**

- **Restored individual summon banner sizes** instead of forcing every banner into the same dimensions.
- **Updated the Standard Summon+ artwork.**
- **Improved Exchange Shop layout on taller screens** and removed unnecessary date labels from permanent shops.
- **Updated News and Updates** with banners for the project’s YouTube channel, Discord and GitHub repository.

**Bug Reports**

- **Improved the report form’s wording and instructions.**
- **Send Report automatically prepares a ZIP** containing game logs, device details and Android information, then opens the email app with the report attached.
- **Added clearer wording for saving the diagnostic ZIP separately.**
