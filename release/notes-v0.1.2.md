# Exvius Offline v0.1.2

This update fixes Quest Claim buttons and the Home menu.

- Rookie and Advanced Quest rewards can now be claimed. Claimed rewards and notification counts update immediately.
- Fixed a Panel Quest reward that failed to pay.
- Vortex is back in the lower carousel and can be opened without waiting for its story unlock.
- Removed the duplicate Expedition shortcut and the unresponsive Anniversary icon.
- Added artwork to the bundle offers that had blank icons.
- Repeat mission rewards now show the Lapis icon and the amount earned.
- Restored the Coin, Fragment and Clash of Wills exchange catalogues, including their item names, prices and stock limits. VIP, EX Coin and Daily Coin shop items now cost Lapis.
- Shortened the opening logos and reduced repeated work while loading game tables and the Vortex menu.
- Reopening the app resumes the running game instead of creating another game screen. Added protection for the Android screenshot crash reported on the test phone.

Install this APK over your current version to keep your save. Your downloaded game data is reused, so this update does not require another full data download.

Known issues: startup took 63 seconds on the test phone. The old TAP graphic on Home and some exchange header artwork still need fixes.
