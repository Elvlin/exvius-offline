# Exvius Offline v0.1.24

- Equipment and materia chests in explorations are now added to your inventory and stay open (#14).
- Units now gain trust (TMR %) from cleared quests: each party unit has a 30% chance of +1% per clear, shown on the results screen (#13).
- Unit levels after battles now match between the results screen and the unit list; levels no longer roll back (#16, #20).
- Fusing a duplicate now raises Super Trust (a 5★ duplicate gives a 7★ unit +50% STMR), and the first identical fusion sends the Trust Moogle (All 50%) to your message box (#21).
- Autoplay (repeat ×N) now pays each run's gil, EXP and drops once instead of re-paying earlier runs (#20).
- Removed the exploration darkness that left a black line and an unshaded strip on tall screens.
- Kept the existing save format and downloaded data.

Open the game online and accept Android's update prompt to keep your campaign and downloaded data.
