**Exvius Offline v0.1.35**

**Multiple Save Profiles**
- Keep up to **10 separate saves** on one device.

**Fixes**
- Fixed over-leveling with Emperor Gigantaur, which used up the unit without raising the level cap.
- Restored missing awakening data for some characters like Reagen.
- Fixed the Farplane intro replaying every time you entered the area.
- Fixed Clash of Will.
- Fixed a crash when opening the awakening screen for some units.
- Fixed Adam's Augmented Visor not being awarded for an LB finish on Viktor Marchenko.
- Fixed percentage summon tickets being rejected.
- Summon tickets now work on every banner that accepts them, including the **5-star Guaranteed 10+1 Summon Ticket** from Adventurer Rewards.

**New Content**
- Added six crossover events from earlier JP: **Imperial SaGa / SaGa**, **Monster Hunter Explore**, **Bravely series**, **SINoALICE**, **Dragon Quest: Emblem of Roto** and **Trials of Mana**. That's **28 quests and 130 stages**, with their units, TMRs, shops, rewards and summons, all in English.
- Restored **42 Global summon campaigns** that were missing, through the end of service, with their step-ups, guarantees, exchanges and original artwork. This includes **Parasite Eve (Aya Brea)** and **Dragon Quest: The Adventure of Dai**.
- Added missing featured units to existing summons, including **Gilgamesh (FFV)**, **Lady Shantotto** and **Petite Jewel Lehftia**.

**Events**
- Events now open on five categories: **Story Events**, **Raids**, **Farming Events**, **Challenges** and **Explorations**.
- **Theo's Workshop** is now always available under Events.

**Summons and Shops**
- Talking to **Guard Mog** now opens the **Dark Matter Shop**, **Abyss Shop** and **STMR Shop**.
- The **Lapis Replica x500** exchange in Land Swarming with Omega is now limited to **50 per day**, resetting daily.

And many others.
